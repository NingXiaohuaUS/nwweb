N&W Family Farm LLC was founded with a simple belief: the best things come from the land when they're grown with patience, honesty, and heart.

Nestled in Lake County, Illinois, our farm is a place where seasons guide our work and community drives our purpose. Every product we offer is a reflection of that commitment.
